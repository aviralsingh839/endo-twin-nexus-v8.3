The Problem - PCOS Complexity & Longitudinal Health Challenges

PCOS/PCOD Challenges

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is complex endocrine condition affecting many aspects of health. Current challenges include:

  • Diagnosis requires Rotterdam criteria: clinical evaluation + ultrasound + clinical context - requires qualified healthcare professional, not wearable alone
  • Symptoms vary widely, longitudinal tracking important - personal baseline matters more than population average
  • Fragmented health data - need for personalized baselines, longitudinal timeline, physiological state, multimodal data, provenance, uncertainty, model registry
  • Accessibility, affordability, privacy - low-cost Arduino Nano pod MAX30102 MPU6050 DS18B20 GSR 20Hz $CP2, not expensive proprietary hardware, local-first SQLite no cloud upload default
  • 70% undiagnosed? Do not make unsupported prevalence claims - system is research/educational prototype not diagnostic tool

Why Longitudinal Monitoring Matters

Single measurements are noisy. Personal baseline matters more than population average. Core scientific story is NOT today's number, it is personal baseline → time series → change → persistence → recovery → context.

  • What is normal for individual first, then look for persistent deviations from personal baseline - PersonalBaselineEngine mean median std MAD rolling confidence min obs circadian context
  • Rolling windows, persistence detection, trend, change-point, recovery - not reacting to one abnormal reading - 6 scenarios stable baseline gradual deviation persistent deviation temporary disturbance sensor failure recovery
  • Temporal patterns: circadian system, autonomic system, metabolic context, multisystem interaction, chrono-metabolic fingerprinting circadian+autonomic+variability+activity+temp+metabolic+longitudinal
