# ENDO-TWIN V8.6 — Workstation Upgrade

V8.6 turns the V8.5 workstation shell into a richer research-monitoring interface while retaining ENDO-TWIN as the general platform and CHRONO-PCOS as its first disease-specific module.

## User experience
Doctor Workstation:
**Command Center · Patient Registry · Live Signals · CHRONO-PCOS · Ultrasound · Reports · Mobile Link**

Patient Workstation:
**Overview · My Health · Measurements · Timeline · Connect · Reports**

A startup-only DEMO/LIVE choice appears before either workstation opens. The selected mode stays fixed for the session.

## Demo
Demo mode uses named synthetic cases and visible condition/module, synthetic tier, research-risk value, quality and driver fields. These are UI examples, never clinical records or model-validation results.

## Live
Live mode accepts CRC-checked Arduino packets and uses the existing PPG, HRV, IMU, GSR and temperature processing modules. The UI shows quality gates and provenance. Poor PPG can suppress HR/HRV, and SpO2 is displayed only after a stricter quality gate.

## CHRONO-PCOS
The Doctor Workstation includes a dedicated CHRONO-PCOS area and patient-registry condition filter. The synthetic queue can be sorted by research priority, research risk or condition. Live records are not assigned fabricated disease risk.

## Evidence
Ultrasound outputs remain conservative: unsupported anatomy is shown as UNKNOWN rather than invented.

## Build
Canonical launcher:
`./START.sh`

Android:
`./setup_android.sh`
`./build_apks.sh all`

## Safety
This remains an educational research prototype, not a medical device and not clinically validated. Live signal processing is engineering functionality, not proof of measurement accuracy or diagnostic performance.
