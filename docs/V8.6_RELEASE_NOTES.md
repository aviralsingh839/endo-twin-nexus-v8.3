# ENDO-TWIN V8.6.1 — Workstation, Android UI & Science Upgrade

> **Historical record.** This document describes the project as it stood at the time
> of writing. The GSR channel and its hardware were retired afterwards; current
> hardware is the ESP32-S3 wearable with a DS18B20 skin-contact temperature probe and
> it emits the `$CP3` frame. See `docs/WEARABLE_AND_MEGA_BUILD_MANUAL.md`.

V8.6 turns the V8.5 workstation shell into a richer research-monitoring interface while retaining ENDO-TWIN as the general platform and CHRONO-PCOS as its first disease-specific module.

## User experience
Doctor Workstation:
**Command Center · Patient Registry · Live Signals · CHRONO-PCOS · Ultrasound · Reports · Mobile Link**

Patient Workstation:
**Overview · My Health · Measurements · Timeline · Connect · Reports**

A startup-only DEMO/LIVE choice appears before either workstation opens. The selected mode stays fixed for the session.

## Demo
Demo mode uses named synthetic cases and visible condition/module, synthetic tier, research-risk value, quality and driver fields. These are UI examples, never clinical records or model-validation results.

## Live
Live mode accepts CRC-checked Arduino packets and uses the existing PPG, HRV, IMU and temperature processing modules. The UI shows quality gates and provenance. Poor PPG can suppress HR/HRV, and SpO2 is displayed only after a stricter quality gate.

## CHRONO-PCOS
The Doctor Workstation includes a dedicated CHRONO-PCOS area and patient-registry condition filter. The synthetic queue can be sorted by research priority, research risk or condition. Live records are not assigned fabricated disease risk.

## Evidence
Ultrasound outputs remain conservative: unsupported anatomy is shown as UNKNOWN rather than invented.

## Build
Canonical launcher:
`./START.sh`

Android:
`./setup_android.sh`
`./build_apks.sh all`

## Safety
This remains an educational research prototype, not a medical device and not clinically validated. Live signal processing is engineering functionality, not proof of measurement accuracy or diagnostic performance.


## V8.6.1 polish pass

### Reference-inspired UI
The Doctor and Patient desktop workstations now follow the shared case-study design language: muted lavender top header, charcoal/slate surfaces, teal active states, compact status badges, patient avatars, denser review panels and bounded trend charts.

Android Patient and Doctor apps use the same visual language and adapt navigation for large screens with a navigation rail while retaining bottom navigation on phones.

### Scientific hardening
- CHRONO-PCOS no longer produces a disease-specific result from wearable physiology alone.
- Diagnostic context is evidence-gated and distinguishes adult from adolescent pathways when years-post-menarche is available.
- Ultrasound/AMH are not used as adolescent diagnostic criteria.
- The internal contextual score is explicitly heuristic/uncalibrated and not a clinical probability.
- PPG peak detection is more efficient and HRV is withheld when the clean interval count or signal quality is insufficient.
- The educational SpO2 estimator remains quality-gated and explicitly non-clinical.

### Research references
The scientific guardrails are based on the international PCOS guideline and contemporary PPG/ECG HRV evidence. See docs/SCIENCE_GUARDRAILS.md and docs/SCIENTIFIC_METHODS_V8_6_1.md.
